OUR STORY

Each of the four founding partners has a strong link to the alcobev industry, which sparked our initial connections and friendship. We'd often discuss over a drink how, even now, there were significant gaps in the availability of good quality products at many price points and different taste segments.

We often asked, “Why wasn't someone using these consumer insights and developing a ‘House of Brands’ portfolio to cater to these large unmet needs of the alcobev consumers.” The opportunity to create, partner with, and even acquire appropriately priced and crafted brands to different smaller taste segments was there for the taking. A series of such conversations led us to state, "Why wait for someone else to innovate? Why not us?” “If not now, when?” After all, between us we had nearly 100 years of rich, diverse, and complimentary alcobev experience. This simple statement sparked a series of conversations that eventually led to the creation of our company.

That's our story, the essence of which we have imbibed into our company's business vision and corporate culture - named 'World of Brands’ (WoB).
